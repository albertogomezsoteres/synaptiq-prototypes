Corvex Payments office guide
Building: Durham Engineering Office, Floor 2
Owner: Dana Whitfield, Workplace and Platform
Reviewed: October 2026

The restrooms are on the second floor at the east end of the main hallway,
directly across from the Cedar meeting room and beside the east stairwell.
From reception, turn right, pass the kitchen, and continue to the Cedar sign.
An accessible single-user restroom is adjacent to the two main restrooms.
This document applies only to the Durham Engineering Office, Floor 2.
