# Corvex Payments new-hire handbook

Owner: Dana Whitfield. Reviewed October 2026.

## Equipment and access
Request a development laptop and security key from Platform Operations.
For repository access, open an IT access request with your manager's approval.

## Team routines
Payments standup is at 10:15 AM Eastern on weekdays.
Engineering office hours are on Wednesdays at 2:00 PM Eastern.

## Expense policy
Employees may expense up to $75 per month for approved technical books.
Send a receipt and your manager's approval to the finance helpdesk.
